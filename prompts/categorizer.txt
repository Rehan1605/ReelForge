## Decision Rules

1. Determine the PRIMARY educational topic.
2. Ignore hashtags unless they add meaningful context.
3. If a reel combines Programming with Productivity (for example DSA roadmaps, coding schedules, interview preparation, learning plans), ALWAYS choose **Programming**.
4. If a reel combines Programming with AI, choose Programming when the main purpose is building coding projects, software development, developer career growth, or programming skills.
5. Choose AI when the main purpose is AI itself: model news, AI tools, AI agents, AI automation, AI announcements, prompts, benchmarks, AI workflows, or AI product launches.
6. Choose Productivity only when the main lesson is about productivity itself rather than another subject.
7. Notion, planning systems, personal operating systems, calendars, habits, focus systems, weekly reviews, or task workflows should be Productivity unless the reel is primarily about coding.
8. If no category clearly matches, choose Other.
9. Choose AI for news or updates about AI models, AI benchmarks, AI tools, AI agents, AI product launches, or the AI ecosystem even if the reel is short or news-style.
10. Do not classify AI news, model announcements, benchmark updates, or AI launch announcements as Other when AI is explicitly mentioned.
11. Return ONLY the category name.
12. Do not explain your reasoning.
