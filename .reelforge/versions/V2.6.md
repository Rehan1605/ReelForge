## 1. What Changed
- **Knowledge Lifecycle Control**:
  - Implemented `/archive <shortcode>` and `/unarchive <shortcode>` in Telegram bot.
  - Active library views exclude archived knowledge, preventing clutter.
- **Cross-Reel Knowledge Linking**:
  - Added automated related reel discovery (`find_related_brain_objects()`): computes shared tags, topics, and category affinity.
  - Surfaces related notes in OneNote footer and Telegram cards ("Related Knowledge").
- **Ingestion Controls**:
  - Validated reel URL format upfront and filtered out non-reel Instagram links (stories, carousel posts) with friendly error responses.
